Every plan at De Nook comes with the basics done properly and a few extras that make the workday easier.

Everyday Essentials

Included with every visit.

Fibre & backup power

Dedicated fibre internet with UPS and generator backup, so your connection survives the next outage.

Unlimited coffee & tea

Locally roasted coffee, tea and filtered water — all day, every day, no extra charge.

Printing & scanning

Black-and-white and colour printing, scanning and binding for the small jobs that always come up.

Lockers & storage

Secure personal lockers for residents and monthly members leave your gear, take your laptop.

Free parking

On-site parking for members no circling the block looking for space.

Community programs
