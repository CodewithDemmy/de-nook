About De Nook

Built by people who needed this place too.

De Nook started as a shared studio between three friends who got tired of working from noisy cafés. Today it's a home base for Lagos's freelancers, founders and small teams.

Our Story

From one shared room to a floor full of desks.

It started with a designer, a developer and a community organiser sharing one rented room in Yaba — because the cafés around them had patchy Wi-Fi, no privacy for client calls, and power that cut out right when it mattered most.

That one room became De Nook: a full floor where freelancers, founders and small teams come to do focused work, without worrying about the basics. The community grew faster than the desks did, so we kept adding both.

2023

One room, three desks

Our founders start sharing a single rented room in Yaba for client work.

2024

De Nook opens

Doors open with 12 hot desks, one meeting room, and a coffee machine that never turns off.

2025

Studio Row & the Boardroom

Private studios and a second meeting room arrive, alongside our first community workshop series.

2026

Today

28 desks, 4 studios, 2 meeting rooms, and a growing community of 150+ members.
