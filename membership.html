Questions

Before you sign up.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade at any time. Plan changes take effect from your next billing date, so you're never charged twice for the same month.

Is there a registration fee?

No setup or registration fees on any plan. The price on the table is the price you pay — no surprises on your first invoice.

Do you offer discounts for students or NYSC members?

Yes — show a valid student or NYSC ID for 15% off Day Pass and Flex plans. Just mention it when you book or send us a message.

What happens if I don't use all my meeting room hours?

Unused meeting room hours don't roll over to the next month, but you can always book additional hours at the member rate of ₦7,500/hour.

Can I bring a guest?

Flex, Resident and Team Studio plans all include guest day passes — see the table above for how many come with your plan each month.

How do I cancel my membership?

Send us a message at least 7 days before your next billing date. There's no lock-in contract on Day Pass or Flex plans.
